Taking you to a day on the Bosphorus. Istanbul never ceases to mesmerize me. In between chaos and calm, just like the sky and sea here, it stands as a witness to centuries connecting Asia and Europe. It is full of grounding contrasts that remind you to pause and seize the moment. Grateful to be born in such a richly cultured city.
