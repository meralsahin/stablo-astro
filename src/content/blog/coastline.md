It has been a year of awareness, discovery, and self-love. The breath is the constant that accompanies us from birth. A moment to breathe in and breathe out. Humming within, I am here now in this. Rhythmic inner harmony, letting it all outside be as is, accepting all as is. Letting go of the resistance and softening the heart. Just like the breath in flow; letting it flow naturally yet anchoring our attention and intention right within it. It is a gift always with us; that is why it is called present. The gift of life.  

This is a paired piece with Aşk, which offers a view out to the universe. 

Details: 60 x 76 cm oil painting
