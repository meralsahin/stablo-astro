It has been a year of awareness, discovery, and self-love. The breath is the constant that accompanies us from birth. A moment to breathe in and breathe out. Humming within, I am here now in this. Inner harmony, letting it all outside be as is, accepting all as is. Trusting the process, letting go of control, and setting its messengers free. Without reacting to the waves, not thinking about the past nor the future, anchoring the attention to now and its sensations. It is a gift always with us; that is why it is called the present. 

This is a paired piece with Aşk, which offers a view out to the universe. 

Details: 60 x 76 cm oil painting
