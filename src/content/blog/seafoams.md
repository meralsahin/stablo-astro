I love the tiny pink sparkles in this one. You can almost feel a Caretta Caretta peeking out from the sand as white waves hit the Patara shore. It brings back my 16th birthday so vividly, taking a boat to the Sunken City. And the best part: being with my family.

Details: 16 x 26 cm oil painting
