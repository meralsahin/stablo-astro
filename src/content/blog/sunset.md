This piece reminds me of the coastal walks I take with my mum. Her favorite thing to do is simply to walk for hours, and I love joining her. Together, we watch the sunset paint the sky in a palette of purple, pink, and red. And of course, I can't forget our favorite part: sharing a bag of Ayçekirdeği on the swings as the day turns to night.
