This piece is a nod to the coastal walks I take with my mum. She simply loves walking for hours, and I love joining her. Together, we watch the sunset paint the sky in shades of purple, pink, and red. And of course, our favorite ritual: sharing a bag of ayçekirdeği on the swings at the end of our walk. Salty air, salty lips.

Details: 15 x 15 cm oil painting
